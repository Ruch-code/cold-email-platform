🚀 How it works

  1. Scan — enter keywords (e.g. "React, Node, remote") and find matching job opportunities.
  2. Scrape — drop a careers page URL to extract structured job listings automatically.
  3. Boost — upload your resume once; it gets tailored per job description keeping your strongest keywords.
  4. Email — generate and send a personalized cold email to each recruiter.
